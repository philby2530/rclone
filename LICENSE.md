Privacy Policy

Application: rclone2_philby

Last updated: September 23, 2026
Overview

rclone2_philby is a personal application that backs up the user's own photos to the user's own Google Drive account. This policy explains what data the application handles and how it is treated.
What the application accesses

With your authorization via the Google Drive API (OAuth 2.0), rclone2_philby may access:

    The files and photos you ask it to back up;
    Enough information about your Google Drive account to upload and manage those backups.

How data is used

    Photos are copied from your device to your own Google Drive storage.
    Files are only read and written at your explicit request.
    No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data storage and retention

All backed-up content resides in your own Google Drive account. The application does not maintain its own separate database of your files.
Google API Services User Data Policy

rclone2_philby uses Google API Services in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
Your rights

Because all data stays in your own Google Drive account, you can review, download, or delete it at any time directly through Google Drive. You may also revoke the application's access at any time via your Google Account permissions page.
